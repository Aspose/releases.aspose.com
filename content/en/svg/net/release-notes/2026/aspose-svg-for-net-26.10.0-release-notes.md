As per the regular monthly update process of all APIs being offered by Aspose, we are honored to announce the October release of Aspose.SVG for .NET.

### Release Notes

Aspose.SVG for .NET 26.10.0 is published as part of the October monthly release.

**Package references**<br>
Aspose.SVG for .NET 26.10.0 [NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Aspose.Svg)<br>
Aspose.SVG for .NET 26.10.0, SkiaSharp build [NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Aspose.SVG.Drawing.SkiaSharp)<br>

## **Improvements and Changes**

- **SkiaSharp 4.153.1:** The SkiaSharp build of Aspose.SVG for .NET now uses SkiaSharp 4.153.1 (previously 3.116.1).
- **XPS colors in the SkiaSharp build:** Fill and stroke colors in XPS output keep their exact sRGB values with SkiaSharp 4.
